How to read these results

  • HHS_Care = number of children currently in HHS care (care load).
  • Discharged_From_HHS = daily successful sponsor placements (placement / discharge demand).
  • RandomForest and GradientBoosting are the machine-learning models required by the project.
  • ARIMA / ExpSmoothing are pure time-series models useful for multi-day ahead views.
  • Net Pressure is the operational early-warning signal for capacity stress.
